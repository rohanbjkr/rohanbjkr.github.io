Linear Algebra
from Zero

A visual and intuitive journey through vectors, matrices, transformations, and the mathematics behind them.

Start Learning →

Learn linear algebra differently.

Linear algebra can look intimidating at first. Rows of numbers, matrices, vectors, determinants, eigenvalues... But the underlying ideas are surprisingly simple. This course starts from the beginning and builds everything step by step.

💡 The philosophy
Start with intuition. Visualize the idea. Then introduce the mathematics.
