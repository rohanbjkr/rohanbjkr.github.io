# Linear Algebra from Zero

### A visual and intuitive journey through linear algebra

Linear algebra can look intimidating at first. Rows of numbers, matrices, vectors, determinants, eigenvalues...

But the underlying ideas are surprisingly simple.

This course starts from the beginning and builds everything step by step — using intuition, visualization, examples, and mathematics.

## 🚀 Start Learning

### Part 1 — What Is a Vector?

Imagine you are standing at the origin.

You move 3 units to the right and 2 units upward.

We can represent that movement as

\[
\mathbf{v} =
\begin{bmatrix}
3\\
2
\end{bmatrix}
\]

This is a vector.

## Why Learn Linear Algebra?

Linear algebra is one of the foundations of modern quantitative work.

You will encounter it in:

**Economics · Econometrics · Statistics · Machine Learning · Optimization · Data Science**

For example, the idea of **linear independence** eventually helps us understand why perfect multicollinearity creates problems in regression.
